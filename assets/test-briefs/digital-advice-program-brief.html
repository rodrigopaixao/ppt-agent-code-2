1. Why this matters now

Clients increasingly start their banking and investing decisions online, but most still finish them with a person. Today only 18% of our product sales are completed digitally, against an industry leader benchmark of 34%. Our digital channels generate strong interest but lose clients at the point where they need help: 42% of clients who start a digital application and ask for support never complete it.

At the same time, demand for advice is growing. Requests for adviser appointments rose 27% in 2025, while the average wait for a first appointment is 9 days. Clients who are offered an appointment within 2 days convert at more than twice the rate of those who wait longer.

2. Our strategy

The executive team has agreed one aspiration for the program: to be the primary digital partner for our clients. Four pillars support it, and together they are expected to drive sustainable, profitable growth.

  1. Digital-first advice. Guided journeys in the app for everyday needs, with advisers available on video when a client needs a person.
  2. Simple, fair pricing. One fee schedule across every channel, so clients pay the same whether they deal with us online or in person.
  3. Connected client data. A single client view for every team, so advisers see what the client already did online and can offer the next best action in real time.
  4. Faster delivery. Moving from quarterly to monthly releases, with one product team per client journey.

The pillars should be read as a whole: the aspiration sits above them, and profitable growth is the result of delivering all four together.

3. Where the client journey breaks today

We mapped the inbound journey for two groups of clients. In both, the moment that decides the outcome is the hand-off from digital to a person. This is where clients either get an appointment or on-demand support quickly, or abandon.
